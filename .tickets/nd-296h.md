# Test OnePlus audio service ordering effects

Check whether speaker-positive/mic-zero state depends on boot-time or manual ordering of oneplus-audio-route, oneplus-mic-source, PipeWire, and WirePlumber.

## Design

Use current runtime only unless a single reboot handoff is explicitly justified. Compare manual starts/restarts and service states, record whether capture remains zero or becomes non-zero. Keep persistent service changes minimal and reversible.

## Acceptance Criteria

Ordering experiments and readings are recorded; either a reliable order produces positive mic readings without speaker regression, or service-order explanations are ruled out for current runtime.
